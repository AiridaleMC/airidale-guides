# Gangs, Weekly Rewards, and Gang Bosses

## Building Gang Progress

Gang leaderboard progress comes from the mcMMO experience earned by gang members. Every tracked mcMMO skill can contribute.

- `/gang top` shows the current leaderboard.
- `/gang contributions` shows contributions from your gang members.
- `/gang contributions <skill>` filters the list to one mcMMO skill.
- Eligible members of a top-three gang can collect their weekly reward with `/gang claim`.

The weekly leaderboard rolls over every Sunday at 9:00 PM Eastern Time.

## Weekly Gang Rewards

| Place | Reward for each eligible member |
|---|---|
| 1st | $200,000, 1 virtual Epic Crate Key, and temporary access to the `#1 Gang` tag for 7 days |
| 2nd | $100,000 and 1 virtual Rare Crate Key |
| 3rd | $50,000 and 1 virtual Uncommon Crate Key |

Gangs can have up to five members and progress to level 100. Gang Chat unlocks at level 10, Alliances at level 50, and XP Share at level 100.

## Shared Gang Storage

Gang storage is a communal inventory that every gang member can access. Any member can deposit or remove items, so only store something there if you are comfortable sharing it with the entire gang.

Staff will not replace shared items taken by another member or lost after someone leaves the gang.

## Buying Gang Boss Keys

Gang boss keys are physical Nether Star items sold by the Lunch Lady in the Keys & More shop.

| Boss key | Scrap cost | Online, non-AFK gang members required |
|---|---:|---:|
| Contraband Kingpin | 750 | 2 |
| Prison Executioner | 1,000 | 3 |
| Prison Surgeon | 1,000 | 3 |
| Prison Warden | 1,250 | 4 |

These Scrap amounts are the prices of the keys, not guaranteed rewards from the bosses.

## Starting a Gang Boss Fight

- Only the gang leader can activate a boss key.
- Right-click the key, then right-click again when asked to confirm.
- The required number of gang members must be online and not marked AFK.
- Eligible online members are brought into the encounter when it begins.
- Only one gang boss fight can run at a time.
- A fight can last up to 15 minutes.
- The key is kept if the fight cannot start correctly or too few members can enter.
- The fight ends when the boss is defeated, time runs out, or no participants remain.

## Boss Rewards

Gang bosses can drop experience, a Flare, and rare equipment. Eligible participants also receive Souls:

| Boss | Souls |
|---|---:|
| Contraband Kingpin | 6–10 |
| Prison Executioner | 8–14 |
| Prison Surgeon | 8–14 |
| Prison Warden | 12–18 |
