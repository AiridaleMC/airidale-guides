# Enchanting and Soul Costs

## How Soul Costs Work

Enchanting on Airidale uses physical Souls from your inventory. The price of each level is calculated as:

`level's base cost × item's material multiplier`

When buying more than one level at once, calculate each new level separately and add those prices together.

For example, upgrading a Diamond Sword from Looting I to Looting III buys both Looting II and Looting III:

- Looting II: `10 × 6 = 60 Souls`
- Looting III: `20 × 6 = 120 Souls`
- **Total: 180 Souls**

Always check the price shown in the enchanting menu before confirming.

## Material Multipliers

| Material | Multiplier |
|---|---:|
| Leather | ×2 |
| Wooden | ×2 |
| Stone | ×3 |
| Copper | ×4 |
| Golden | ×4 |
| Iron | ×5 |
| Diamond | ×6 |
| Netherite | ×7 |
| Chainmail | ×1 |
| Turtle | ×1 |
| Bow, Crossbow, Fishing Rod, Shield, or Mace | ×1 |

## Enchantment Conflicts

The enchanter will not combine:

- Sharpness, Smite, and Bane of Arthropods with one another.
- Fortune and Silk Touch with one another.

## Reading the Cost Tables

The tables below show the **base cost of each individual level** before the material multiplier. A row such as `1 / 3 / 7` means level I costs 1 base Soul, level II costs 3, and level III costs 7.

Only compatible item and enchantment combinations appear in the enchanting menu.

## Prestige Enchanting

Prestige Enchanting unlocks at P5. Right-click an enchanting table for ordinary enchanting or shift-right-click it to open the Prestige Enchanter.

The Prestige Enchanter sells only the specific higher levels shown below. Tiered items still use their material multiplier; Bows and Fishing Rods use ×1.

### Prestige Armor

The following upgrades are available independently for Helmets, Chestplates, Leggings, and Boots:

| Enchantment offered | Base Soul cost |
|---|---:|
| Fire Protection V | 37 |
| Blast Protection V | 37 |
| Projectile Protection V | 37 |

Protection V is not offered. Do not assume an enchantment or level is available unless it appears in the Prestige Enchanter.
