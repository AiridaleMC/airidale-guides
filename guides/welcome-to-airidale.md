# Welcome to Airidale

## A Classic Prison Server

Airidale is a classic prison server where progress is earned one ward at a time. You begin in E Ward, mine and complete quests for money, rank up through increasingly difficult wards, and eventually unlock prestige progression.

Airidale was founded in 2017 by Kobe, also known as InvalidZ. JohnnyGooodTime joined as co-owner in May 2018 and became the sole owner in October 2022. Today, Airidale is owned by JohnnyGooodTime and his wife, CamGooodTime.

Longtime players may also recognize JohnnyGooodTime as JohnnyACosta, DevTechGames, or Dev003, and CamGooodTime as CamIsAQTPie.

## Your Path Through the Prison

Airidale has five main prison wards: E, D, C, B, and A. Each ward contains four ranks, so your complete path is:

`E4 → E3 → E2 → E1 → D4 → D3 → D2 → D1 → C4 → C3 → C2 → C1 → B4 → B3 → B2 → B1 → A4 → A3 → A2 → A1`

Everyone starts at E4. A1 is the highest regular rank and is where prestige progression begins.

## What You Will Find in Each Ward

Every ward gives you new places to explore and new ways to progress, including:

- Rentable cells.
- A regular mine containing ores.
- A wood mine.
- Passive and hostile mob farms.
- Daily and weekly quests.

Quests can award money and other useful rewards while giving you another reason to explore each ward.

## Prestige Progression

After reaching A1, you can prestige and begin the rank journey again from E4. Airidale currently has 10 prestige levels.

Your first prestige unlocks the prestige area, which includes its own mine, wood mine, and rentable cafeteria cells. These rentable spaces may also be described in game as prestige shops.
