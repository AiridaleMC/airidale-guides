# Crates

Crate keys can be earned in various ways on Airidale, and can be opened at `/warp crates`. You can view your keys by doing `/keys`. You can also withdraw these keys into actual items by doing `/keys withdraw <key>`, which can be useful to sell them to others players for in-game money.

- **Left Click** the crate to use one virtual key or the key item in your hand.
- **Crouch + Left Click** to open up to 10 keys at once.
- **Right Click** the crate to preview its current rewards and displayed chances.

## Vote Crate

Keys for Vote Crates can be obtained by voting for Airidale on 5 websites. These websites can be opened in your browser by doing `/vote`. Each website usually allows voting once every 24 hour.

Make sure to use your in-game username when voting! These keys are delivered automatically, when the vote provider confirms your vote based on the username given to the site's vote form. If you are offline, eligible rewards are queued for your next login.

Opening a Vote Crate will reward you with one of the following regular crate keys:

| Reward | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| Common Key | 900/1000 (90%) |
| Uncommon Key | 90/1000 (9%) |
| Rare Key | 7/1000 (0.7%) |
| Epic Key | 2/1000 (0.2%) |
| Legendary Key | 1/1000 (0.1%) |

## mcMMO Crates

Keys for mcMMO Crates can be obtained by doing certain mcMMO actions, by fishing, mining, killing mobs or woodcutting. Each of these actions has a chance to drop a crate key, which can be used to open the corresponding mcMMO Crate. mcMMO Crates can be opened at `/warp crates`, and interacting with Crazy Carl. These keys cannot be withdrawn into items, and are only usable by the player who obtained them.

## Tag Crate

Keys for Tag Crates can be obtained by opening then from Regular Crates, or by purchasing them from Lunch Lady in the Cafeteria.

Opening a Tag Crate will reward you with 1 out of 69 tags, ranging from Common to Epic rarity. Due to their quantity, the tags are not listed here. You can view all available tags by left clicking the Tag Crate at `/warp crates`.

## Collector's Crates

Keys for Collector's Crates can be obtained by opening them from Legendary Crates, or by prestiging Fishing, Mining or Swords mcMMo abilities. Unlike other crates, Collector's Crates can be found at `/warp library` behind Miss Golden's desk.

Opening a Collector's Crate will reward you with 1 out of 30 Collector's (vanity) items, ranging from Rare to Mythic rarity. Due to their quantity, the items are not listed here. You can view all available Collector's items by left clicking the Collector's Crate.
