# Complete Player Guide

## Start with the Help Menu

Use `/helpmenu` whenever you are unsure where to find a feature. It connects you to the server's main player guides and menus.

Useful shortcuts include:

| Command | Opens |
|---|---|
| `/rules` | Server rules |
| `/bosses` | Ward boss guide |
| `/toggle` | Personal settings |
| `/boosts` | Active mcMMO boost timers |
| `/ptop` | Prestige leaderboard |

The website has dedicated guides for [Ward Quests](/guides/ward-quests), [Enchanting and Soul Costs](/guides/enchanting-and-soul-costs), and the [Referral Program](/guides/referral-program). Cell information is covered below.

Some shops are opened by NPCs instead of commands. Visit the Lunch Lady for quest-reset vouchers, Totems, keys, and other Scrap purchases.

## Rules and Fair Play

Use `/rules` for the complete in-game presentation. The core expectations are:

1. Do not use hacked or modified clients that provide an unfair advantage. OptiFine is allowed.
2. Do not exploit glitches, leave the prison map, or duplicate items.
3. Do not advertise other servers or communities.
4. Do not use alternate accounts.
5. Going AFK is at your own risk.
6. Keep chat appropriate and respectful.
7. Staff decisions are final.
8. Do not ask staff members for a staff rank; use the official application process.
9. Use English in public chat so it can be moderated.
10. Do not trade in-game items for real-world money.
11. Item names may use no more than two magic-character sequences.

If you are unsure whether something is allowed, open a Discord support ticket before doing it.

## Ranking Up and Prestiging

Right-click the Warden near spawn to rank up or prestige. Your path begins at E4 and ends at A1 before prestige.

Before entering a new ward:

- Empty your old ward cell. The cell is released during the ward change.
- Finish quests in the ward you are leaving because their progress is reset.
- Check the exact live rank price before confirming.

Donor and prestige cells are not released during an ordinary ward change.

See the [Ranks and Rankup Costs](/guides/ranks-and-rankups) and [Prestige Guide](/guides/prestige-guide) for the full progression details.

## Cells and Prestige Shops

Regular ward cells are tied to your ward progress and are rented for seven days at a time.

Prestige shops, also called prestige cells, are rentable spaces in the cafeteria prestige area. They require P1, cost $100,000 for seven days, and measure `3 × 2 × 3` blocks.

Donor cells begin at Knight rank and remain available after prestige. Visit `/warp donate` to explore the donor area and preview current donor benefits.

When any ward cell expires or is released, items inside its containers are moved to that ward's Auction House.

## Enchanting, Repairs, and Custom Tools

Airidale enchanting uses physical Souls from your inventory, and costs vary by enchantment, level, and item material.

After unlocking Prestige Enchanting at P5, shift-right-click the ordinary enchanting table to open it. Read [Enchanting and Soul Costs](/guides/enchanting-and-soul-costs) before spending Souls.

Anvils outside spawn can repair and rename items. Repair prices depend on material and remaining durability.

Airidale custom tools are unbreakable Netherite tools available from crates or the store. Visit `/warp crates` to find the crate area, and see [Common Player Questions](/guides/common-questions) for each custom tool effect.

## Gangs

Gang leaderboard progress comes from mcMMO experience earned by members. The leaderboard rolls over Sunday at 9:00 PM Eastern Time.

- `/gang top` shows the leaderboard.
- `/gang contributions` shows member contributions.
- `/gang claim` collects an eligible weekly reward.
- Gangs can have up to five members and reach level 100.
- Gang Chat unlocks at level 10, Alliances at level 50, and XP Share at level 100.
- Gang storage is shared; every member can remove stored items.

Read [Gangs and Bosses](/guides/gangs-and-bosses) before buying or activating a gang boss key.

Use `/toggle` to manage your personal settings. Available options include:

- Junker Chat and Coinflip broadcasts.
- Scoreboard and inventory-full alerts.
- Automatic ward effects and mcMMO ability alerts.

Each setting has a short anti-spam delay before it can be changed again.

## Creating a Player Shop

Player shops use a chest and a nearby sign:

1. Place a chest and put the item you want to trade inside it.
2. Place a sign one block away, such as directly above the chest.
3. Leave line 1 blank; your name is filled automatically.
4. Put the number of items per transaction on line 2.
5. Put the buy and/or sell prices on line 3.
6. Put `?`, the item name, or the item ID on line 4.

Example—the first line is intentionally blank:

```text

64
B 10:S 5
?
```

This shop lets another player buy 64 items for $10 or sell 64 items to the shop for $5.

### Price Formats

- `B 10` means players can buy from the shop for $10.
- `S 5` means players can sell to the shop for $5.
- `B 10:S 5` supports both directions.

Use `/iteminfo` while holding an item if you need its exact shop name. Look at an existing shop and use `/shopinfo` to inspect its owner, item, prices, and status.

If players cannot buy, check the chest's stock and make sure line 3 includes a `B` price. If they cannot sell, check for empty chest space and an `S` price.
