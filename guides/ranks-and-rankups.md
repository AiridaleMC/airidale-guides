# Ranks and Rankup Costs

## How Ranking Up Works

You begin at E4 and work through 20 regular ranks before reaching A1. The prices below are the base costs for players who have not prestiged. Prestige multipliers can increase these prices on later playthroughs.

Each listed price is the amount needed to reach that rank, not a running total.

## Complete Rank Path

`E4 → E3 → E2 → E1 → D4 → D3 → D2 → D1 → C4 → C3 → C2 → C1 → B4 → B3 → B2 → B1 → A4 → A3 → A2 → A1`

A1 is the highest regular rank. Once you reach it, you can begin prestige progression.

## What Happens When You Enter a New Ward

Moving from E1 to D4, D1 to C4, C1 to B4, or B1 to A4 brings you into a new ward. When this happens:

- Your cell in the ward you are leaving is released.
- Your weekly quest progress for the ward you are leaving is reset.

Empty your old cell and finish any weekly quest progress you want to keep before crossing into the next ward.
