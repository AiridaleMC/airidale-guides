# NPC Locations

## E Ward Quest NPCs

### David Leatherman — Butcher I

Find David in the E Ward animal area, to the left of the entrance.

### Marty — Miscellaneous I

Marty is near the E Ward shop, to the right of the ward entrance.

### Tim Ber — Wood I

Tim Ber is just inside E Ward on the left. Continue past the animal area toward the wood area.

### Scarr — Slayer I

Scarr is in the mob cave near the mine at the back of E Ward.

### Sacul — Mining I

Sacul is near the mine at the back of E Ward.

### Ender Eddie — Weekly I and Deliveries

Ender Eddie starts Weekly I and accepts the Butcher I and Wood I deliveries.

Enter the E Ward animal area, go down the well, and follow the maze at the bottom.

## E Ward Smuggler's Den

The Smuggler's Den brokers are at the bottom of the well. They sell temporary bulk-selling passes that pay double the normal rate for selected materials. These passes are separate from the Prestige 1 commissary.

| Broker | Pass price | Materials bought at double value |
|---|---:|---|
| Hector | 333 Scrap | Gold, Iron, and Copper |
| Shane | 666 Scrap | Diamond, Gold, and Copper |
| Mia | 999 Scrap | Emerald, Gold, and Iron |

## D Ward Quest NPCs

### Hyim — Mining II

Hyim is immediately inside D Ward on the left. His name is spelled **H-Y-I-M**.

### Scuba Steve — Miscellaneous II

Continue past Hyim and look to the right.

### Zygmunt — Slayer II

Zygmunt is to the right of Scuba Steve, just outside the D Ward mob cave.

### Eden — Butcher II

Eden is about halfway toward the back of D Ward on the left, outside the animal farm.

### Marley — Wood II

Marley is just past Eden, outside the wood area and in front of the Auction House.

### Dirt-Block Donnie — Weekly II and Wood Delivery

Dirt-Block Donnie starts Weekly II and accepts the Wood II delivery.

After entering D Ward, take the stairs on the left. Enter the cell marked **Donnie** on its rent sign, then walk through the picture-frame false door into the sewers.

## C Ward Quest NPCs

### Benny — Butcher III

Benny is immediately inside C Ward on the left, near the water.

### Jeffrey — Wood III

Walk straight ahead from the C Ward entrance. Jeffrey is outside the wood area.

### Frank — Slayer III

Frank is left of Jeffrey at the entrance to the mob area.

### Sam — Mining III

Sam is on the right side of the large cell cave.

### Hooper — Miscellaneous III

Hooper is immediately inside C Ward on the right, near the hoppers.

### Cipher — Weekly III and Deliveries

Cipher starts Weekly III and accepts the Miscellaneous III and Wood III deliveries.

From the C Ward entrance, turn left and then left again. Enter the cave behind the waterfall to reach the sewers.

## B Ward Quest NPCs

### Robert — Mining IV

Robert is in an igloo just inside B Ward on the left.

### Thomas — Butcher IV

Thomas is straight ahead under the large tree. This route enters a PvP area, so carry only what you are willing to risk.

### Ned — Slayer IV

Ned is behind Thomas, just inside the entrance to the mob-area cave.

### Charlie — Miscellaneous IV

Charlie is to the right of the mob-area entrance near the snowman. This is also inside the PvP area.

### Jimothy — Wood IV

Jimothy is in the B Ward wood mine, just beyond the animal area.

### Bedrock Benny — Weekly IV and Deliveries

Bedrock Benny starts Weekly IV and accepts the Miscellaneous IV and Wood IV deliveries.

After entering B Ward, turn right and then right again. Look for a cave-like opening that drops into the sewers.

## A Ward Quest NPCs

### Cupid — Butcher V

Cupid is in the animal area to the right after entering A Ward.

### Al — Miscellaneous V and Delivery

Al starts Miscellaneous V and accepts its delivery. Turn left after entering A Ward and go up the stairway opposite Cupid.

### Post Manole — Wood V

Walk straight into A Ward toward the obsidian portal. Post Manole is about halfway back on the left, before the portal.

### Paul — Mining V

Paul is opposite Post Manole on the right, before the obsidian portal.

### Jereas — Slayer V

Walk through the obsidian portal and look to the right.

### Alleyway Artie — Weekly V and Wood Delivery

Alleyway Artie starts Weekly V and accepts the Wood V delivery. Enter the sewers through the opening behind the waterfall in the A Ward animal area.

## Important Spawn NPCs

### Warden — Ranks and Prestige

The Warden stands just outside spawn. Right-click the Warden to open the rank and prestige interface.

### Airi — Player Help

Airi is at spawn. Right-click Airi to ask the server's AI assistant a question. The correct name is **Airi**, although older text may use the spelling “Aery.”

### Kino and Yard — Mob Arenas

Kino and Yard are together on the left side of spawn. Use these NPCs to enter their respective wave-based mob arenas.

## Cafeteria NPCs

### Lunch Lady — Scrap Shops

The Lunch Lady is across from Kino and Yard. Her shops include:

- Souls for 100 Scrap each.
- Mining, Wood, Butcher, and Slayer quest-reset vouchers.
- Netherite Upgrade Smithing Templates for 250 Scrap.
- Totems.
- Tag Crate Keys for 250 Scrap.
- Gang boss keys.

Quest-reset voucher prices vary by quest and tier.

### Matt — Gamble Potions

Matt is on the cafeteria's second floor above the Lunch Lady. He sells potions that give you a chance to win money when consumed.

### Phil — Scrap Dealer

Phil is also on the cafeteria's second floor. His Scrap Dealer interface lets you gamble Scrap.

## Commissary and Junker NPCs

### Commissary Guard

The Commissary Guard is left of spawn near the entrance to the prestige commissary.

### Ward Junkers

Every regular and donor ward has a Junker NPC. Junkers recycle accepted unwanted items for money and return protected materials instead of destroying them.

The NPC skins honor players who won contests during the season. See the Junker and Auto-Junker guide for payouts, protected items, milestones, and Auto-Junker details.
