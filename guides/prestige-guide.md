# Prestige Guide

## Before You Prestige

You can prestige after reaching A1. There are 10 prestige levels, from P1 through P10.

Prestiging begins a new rank journey and makes several permanent changes to your current progress. Before confirming, make sure you understand what will reset and move anything you want to keep out of your A Ward cell.

## What Resets

Every prestige:

- Returns your regular prison rank to E4.
- Resets your money balance.
- Releases your A Ward cell and sends its stored container items to the A Ward Auction House.
- Resets all of your quest progress.
- Returns you to spawn.
- Clears your active effects.

Donor and prestige cells are not released by this reset.

## What You Receive

Every prestige awards Scrap and one virtual Legendary Crate Key. Higher prestige levels also improve daily access to the prestige commissary and reduce the cooldown of Haste Totems.

| P3 | 1,200 | 3 minutes | 27 minutes | — |
| P4 | 1,300 | 4 minutes | 26 minutes | — |
| P5 | 1,400 | 5 minutes | 25 minutes | Prestige Enchanting |
| P6 | 1,500 | 6 minutes | 24 minutes | — |
| P7 | 1,600 | 7 minutes | 23 minutes | — |

Use `/commissary cooldown` to check your remaining prestige-commissary access time.

## Important Perk Unlocks

### Prestige 1

P1 unlocks:

- The Prestige chat tag.
- Command access to your ordinary Ender Chest with `/enderchest` or `/ec`.
- The prestige area, including the timed commissary and rentable prestige cells or shops.

### Prestige 2

P2 unlocks Auto-Junker through `/junker auto`. Auto-Junker automatically recycles ordinary items you add to its filter and pays their normal Junker value.

Protected items and items with names, lore, enchantments, damage, or other special data cannot be added to the filter.

### Prestige 5

P5 unlocks Prestige Enchanting. Shift-right-click the normal enchanting table to open the Prestige Enchanter.

Prestige Enchanting offers specific higher enchantment levels rather than unlimited upgrades. Review the Enchanting and Soul Costs guide before spending Souls.

## Rank Prices After Prestiging

Rank prices increase after prestige, but the available reference information disagrees about the exact multiplier for each level. For an accurate price, trust the amount shown by the live rankup interface before confirming.

The Ranks and Rankup Costs guide lists the base P0 prices for comparison.
