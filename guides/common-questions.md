# Common Player Questions

## Special Pickaxes and Axes

### What does a Bountiful Pickaxe do?

It gives you the best adjacent block around the block you mine.

### What does an Explosive Pickaxe do?

It breaks a `3 × 3 × 1` area at once.

### What does an Inventory Pickaxe do?

It sends mined blocks directly to your inventory.

### What does a Smelter's Pickaxe do?

It smelts compatible blocks as you mine them. For example, iron ore produces iron ingots instead of raw iron.

### What do Bedrock or plain Unbreakable tools do?

Bedrock Pickaxes and Axes are unbreakable but do not have another special effect. Other named Airidale tools can be unbreakable and still include effects such as Bountiful, Explosive, Inventory, Smelter's, or Lumber.

### What does a Lumber Axe do?

It breaks up to a `3 × 3 × 3` area of wood or logs.

## Scrap and the Lunch Lady

### What is Scrap used for?

Scrap is a special currency used mainly at the Lunch Lady's shops. It can also be gambled with the Phil NPC.

### What can I buy from the Lunch Lady?

Her current Scrap purchases include:

- Souls for 100 Scrap each.
- Mining, Wood, Butcher, and Slayer quest-reset vouchers. Prices depend on the quest level.
- Netherite Upgrade Smithing Templates for 250 Scrap.
- Totems.
- One Tag Crate Key for 250 Scrap.
- Physical gang boss keys costing between 750 and 1,250 Scrap.

There is currently no Miscellaneous or Weekly quest-reset voucher.

## Souls and Enchanting

### What are Souls used for?

Souls are the currency used to enchant equipment on Airidale. They are physical items, so they must be in your inventory when you enchant something.

Place an item into an enchanting table and hover over an enchantment to see its Soul cost before buying it.

### Where can I get Souls?

Souls can come from mining, woodcutting, killing mobs or animals, fishing, and crates. You can also trade with other players or buy Souls from the Lunch Lady for 100 Scrap each.

## Disenchanting Safely

### What is the difference between safe and risky disenchanting?

- **Safe disenchanting** removes the enchantments and returns your item. It does not give you enchantment books.
- **Risky disenchanting** always destroys your item. It may return some enchantments as Soulbound Books, depending on the item's material.

Soulbound Books can be applied to compatible equipment for an experience cost. Never use risky disenchanting on an item you are not prepared to lose.

## Upgrading Diamond Gear to Netherite

### What do I need for a Netherite upgrade?

Use a smithing table with:

- A Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template from the Lunch Lady.
- One Netherite Ingot.
- The Diamond tool or armor piece being upgraded.
- Enough Souls to cover the material-price difference for its enchantments.

## Donor Kits

### How often can I claim a donor kit?

Each donor kit can be claimed once every seven days.

## Mine Progression

### Which regular ward mines are better?

Regular mines improve from E Mine through D, C, B, and finally A Mine.

### Which donor mines are better?

Donor mines improve in this order: Journeyman, Noble, Knight, Elder, Hero, and Legend. A Mine is roughly comparable to Knight Mine, while Elder, Hero, and Legend are stronger.

### Do the mines contain Diamond or Emerald Blocks?

No. Diamond Ore and Emerald Ore are the best materials found in regular and donor mines.

## Smugglers

### How much do smugglers pay?

Each smuggler pays twice the normal server-shop price for the items that smuggler accepts.

## Crate Keys

### Where are my crate keys stored?

Fishing, Mining, Mob, Woodcutting, and Legendary keys are virtual. Use `/keys` to view them.

Tag Crate Keys and gang boss keys bought from the Lunch Lady are physical items and must remain in your inventory.

### Where do I open crates?

Visit `/warp crates` to find the crate area.

## Ward Auction Houses

### What happens to items when I lose or leave a cell?

Items stored in cell containers, including chests and barrels, are moved to that ward's Auction House when the cell expires, is released, or is taken during a ward change.

Each ward has its own Auction House. Look for the large `AH` letters above its entrance.

## Interactive Chat

### How do I show an item or inventory in chat?

- `[item]` shares the item in your main hand.
- `[inventory]` shares your inventory.
- `[ender]` or `[e]` shares your Ender Chest.

These tags only create an interactive preview; they do not give another player your items.

## Ward Effects

### How do permanent ward effects work?

In every ward, reaching level 2 grants a permanent Speed effect and reaching level 1 grants permanent Haste I. Use `/effects` to turn your unlocked ward effects on or off.

## Prestige Ender Chest Access

### What command does Prestige 1 unlock?

Prestige 1 unlocks command access to your ordinary Ender Chest through `/enderchest` or `/ec`.

## Mob Arenas

### How do Kino and Yard work?

Kino and Yard are wave-based mob arenas that progress from wave 1 through wave 100. Rewards include Scrap and crate keys.

A full solo completion can take around two hours, so plan enough time before starting.

## Discord Support and Rules

### How do I contact staff?

Use `/discord` to join the Airidale Discord, then create a ticket in the support area.

### Where can I read the rules?

Use `/rules` to open the in-game rules. If a situation is unclear, ask staff in a Discord ticket before taking a risk.
