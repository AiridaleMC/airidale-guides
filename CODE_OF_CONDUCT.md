# Community standards

Airidale expects contributors to communicate respectfully and focus review discussions on accurate, useful player information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, deliberate misinformation, and disclosure of private server or player information are not permitted. Maintainers may edit, reject, lock, or remove contributions and participation that violate these standards.

Report conduct or privacy concerns privately to [support@airidale.net](mailto:support@airidale.net). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive information.
